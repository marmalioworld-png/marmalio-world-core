# Prompt 02 — Experience Inventory

## Purpose

This is the foundation of the entire system. Every later step — resume, cover letter, LinkedIn, interview prep, negotiation, career-change narrative — draws only from what gets recorded here. Nothing enters this document unless you actually typed it.

## Required Inputs

- Time and willingness to answer the questions honestly and specifically. Rough notes are fine; polish happens in later steps, not here.
- A list of the roles, projects, or experiences you want to inventory (can be as short as one role if you're short on time).

## The Prompt

```
You are helping me build a factual inventory of my work experience. This is the foundation document for everything else I'll do — it must contain only things I actually tell you, nothing else.

Use only the information I provide in this conversation. Do not invent, assume, exaggerate, or infer any employer, title, date, responsibility, metric, or outcome I have not stated. If I mention something vaguely, ask a clarifying question instead of filling in a plausible-sounding detail. If I don't know an exact number (e.g., "increased sales" but no percentage), record it as-is and mark it [METRIC NOT PROVIDED] rather than estimating one.

For each role, project, or experience I describe, ask me in order:
1. Employer/organization name, title, and dates (or "project" if not a formal role).
2. What was I actually responsible for, day to day?
3. What did I work on, build, fix, or deliver that I'm proud of or that took real effort?
4. Do I have any numbers, results, or outcomes attached to that work? (If I don't know, say so — don't guess.)
5. What tools, systems, or skills did I actually use?
6. Is there anything about this role I'd rather downplay or that I'm unsure how to describe? (This helps me flag it for extra care later, not to hide it.)

After I answer for one role, summarize what I told you in a clean, organized entry, then ask if I want to add another role. Do not add anything I did not say. If I correct something, update the entry — do not keep the old version.
```

## Expected Output

One structured entry per role/project, containing only what you stated, with `[METRIC NOT PROVIDED]` or `[NEEDS INPUT]` markers wherever you didn't have a specific detail. No adjectives like "successfully" or "significantly" unless you used them or the outcome you described clearly supports them.

## Truth Check

Read every sentence in the inventory and ask: "Did I actually say this, in these words or clearly this meaning?" If any sentence surprises you, it doesn't belong.

## Refinement Step

```
Go through the inventory entry by entry. For each sentence, tell me whether it came directly from something I said, or whether you added or inferred it. Flag anything you added so I can decide whether to keep, correct, or delete it.
```
