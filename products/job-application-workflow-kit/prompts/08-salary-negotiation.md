# Prompt 08 — Salary Negotiation Preparation

## Purpose

Prepare negotiation talking points and scripts based on market data you have researched yourself and the value you've already documented — never on invented salary figures.

## Required Inputs

- Market-range data you have researched yourself, in your own currency, from sources appropriate to your market (e.g., Glassdoor and levels.fyi in the USA; Glassdoor Canada in Canada; Glassdoor UK or Reed in the UK; Seek or Glassdoor Australia in Australia — see `00b-market-conventions.md` for the full list) for this role, location, and level. This system does not look this up or estimate it for you.
- Your Experience Inventory highlights (Prompt 02 output).
- Any offer you've already received, if applicable (amount, and what's included — base, bonus, equity, benefits).

## The Prompt

```
You are helping me prepare for a salary negotiation. Use only the market data and experience details I provide below — do not state or estimate a salary figure that I have not given you.

Market range data I have researched myself, in my own currency: [PASTE YOUR RESEARCHED RANGE, CURRENCY, AND SOURCE, e.g., "58,000-66,000 GBP base, per Glassdoor UK for this title/location" or "75,000-88,000 USD base, per levels.fyi for this title/location"]

My relevant experience highlights: [PASTE RELEVANT PROMPT 02 SECTIONS]

Current offer, if any: [PASTE OFFER DETAILS OR WRITE "no offer yet"]

Produce:
1. A negotiation opening script that states my target range (using only the range I gave you) and connects it to two or three specific value points from my experience.
2. A response script for if the employer's first offer is below my stated range.
3. A short worksheet of 3–5 questions to ask myself to define my walk-away criteria (minimum acceptable total compensation, non-negotiable benefits, timeline pressure).

Do not suggest a specific dollar figure anywhere that I did not provide. If I ask you to suggest a target range yourself, refuse and explain that this requires my own market research, not an estimate.
```

## Expected Output

A negotiation opening script, a lowball-response script, and a walk-away-criteria worksheet — every dollar figure in the output traceable to what you supplied.

## Truth Check

Scan the output for any number that doesn't match what you provided. A fabricated salary figure here is the highest-stakes possible fabrication in this entire system — check this output more carefully than any other.

## Refinement Step

```
List every dollar figure or percentage that appears in this output, and tell me exactly where each one came from in what I gave you. If any figure doesn't trace back to something I provided, remove it and replace it with [FIGURE NEEDED FROM CANDIDATE].
```
