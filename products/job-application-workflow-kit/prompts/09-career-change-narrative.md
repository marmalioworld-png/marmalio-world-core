# Prompt 09 — Career-Change Narrative

## Purpose

Build an honest transferable-skills narrative for a career change — one that clearly distinguishes what genuinely transfers from your prior field, what transfers partially, and what you are still building toward. This is not a way to imply direct experience you don't have.

## Required Inputs

- Your Experience Inventory from your prior field (Prompt 02 output).
- The target field's requirements (Prompt 01 output for a representative posting in the new field).

## The Prompt

```
You are helping me build a career-change narrative. Use only my prior-field experience below — do not imply I have direct experience in the target field that I do not have.

My prior-field experience inventory:
"""
[PASTE PROMPT 02 OUTPUT HERE]
"""

Target field's requirements (from a representative job posting):
"""
[PASTE PROMPT 01 OUTPUT HERE]
"""

Produce three clearly separated lists:
1. DIRECTLY TRANSFERABLE — skills or experience from my prior field that genuinely, directly apply to the target field's requirements. Explain the connection honestly; do not overstate it.
2. PARTIALLY TRANSFERABLE — skills or experience that relate but would need adaptation or additional context to be relevant. Be specific about what the gap is.
3. GAPS TO ACKNOWLEDGE — target-field requirements I clearly do not yet have covered by my prior experience. Do not suggest ways to word around these gaps; list them plainly so I can decide how to address them (e.g., a course, a project, direct honesty in an interview).

Then write a short (100–150 word) narrative paragraph I can adapt for a cover letter or interview, that honestly frames my transition using only items from list 1 and 2 — never implying list 3 items are already covered.
```

## Expected Output

Three clearly labeled lists and one honest narrative paragraph that never implies experience from the gaps list.

## Truth Check

Read the narrative paragraph on its own, without the lists. Would someone reading it reasonably conclude you have direct experience in the target field? If so, it's overstating — even if no single sentence is technically false, the overall impression matters.

## Refinement Step

```
Reread the narrative paragraph and tell me if an average reader would come away believing I have more direct target-field experience than I actually do. If so, rewrite it to be clearer that this is a transition, not a continuation.
```
