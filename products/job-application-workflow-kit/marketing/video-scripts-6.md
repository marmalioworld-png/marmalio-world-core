# Six Short-Form Video Scripts

Format: 30–45 seconds each, vertical (9:16), faceless (text cards + screen-recording style visuals, no on-camera talent required). Reusable across Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts with minor caption/hashtag adjustments per platform; script 6 is written specifically for LinkedIn's more text-forward native video style.

All claims in these scripts follow the same rules as the product itself: no "beat ATS," no "guaranteed interview," no invented statistics.

---

## Script 1 — Problem/pain hook: "Your resume isn't the problem"

**Hook (0–3s):** "Your resume isn't the problem. Your prompt is."

**Body (3–30s):**
"Most people ask ChatGPT to 'improve my resume' and get back generic buzzwords — 'results-driven,' 'proven track record' — that could describe literally anyone. That's not a resume problem. That's a process problem. You need to give the AI your *real* experience first, in detail, before it tailors anything. One prompt can't do that. A system can."

**CTA (30–40s):** "I built a 9-step system that does this properly — link in bio."

**On-screen text cues:** "Your resume isn't the problem" → "'Results-driven professional'" (crossed out) → "It's a PROCESS problem" → "9-step system, link in bio"

## Script 2 — Before/after demo

**Hook (0–3s):** "Watch this resume bullet before and after."

**Body (3–35s):**
"Before: 'Spearheaded a transformative initiative that dramatically improved operational efficiency.' Sounds impressive. Means nothing. After, using the same facts: 'Led company-wide POS rollout, training 22 staff in two weeks.' Same person, same facts — just specific instead of vague. That's the entire difference this system makes: not bigger claims, more honest, checkable ones."

**CTA (35–40s):** "Full before/afters and the system that builds them — link in bio."

**On-screen text cues:** show both bullets as text cards side by side, "BEFORE" in red, "AFTER" in green.

## Script 3 — Listicle hook: "3 things your resume prompt is missing"

**Hook (0–3s):** "3 things your 'ChatGPT resume prompt' is missing."

**Body (3–35s):**
"One: a step that actually captures your real experience before anything gets written. Two: a truth check, so the AI can't quietly invent a number you never gave it. Three: tailoring to the *specific* posting, not a generic version of your job title. Most one-off prompts skip all three."

**CTA (35–40s):** "All three, built in — link in bio."

**On-screen text cues:** numbered list appearing one at a time: "1. Real experience first" / "2. A truth check" / "3. Tailored to THIS posting"

## Script 4 — Myth-busting: "No, AI can't guarantee you an interview"

**Hook (0–3s):** "No, AI can't guarantee you an interview. Here's what it actually can do."

"Anyone promising 'guaranteed interviews' from a resume tool is lying to you — no document controls what an employer decides. What a good process *can* do: make sure your real experience is clearly, honestly, and specifically presented for the exact role you're applying to. That's a real, achievable thing. That's what this is."

**CTA (35–40s):** "Honest positioning, real workflow — link in bio."

**On-screen text cues:** "❌ 'Guaranteed interviews'" → "✅ Clear, honest, tailored materials"

This system has a dedicated career-change workflow: it separates what directly transfers, what partially transfers, and what's a real gap — then helps you write a narrative that's honest about the transition instead of pretending it isn't one."

**CTA (35–40s):** "If you're changing careers right now, this step alone is worth it — link in bio."

**On-screen text cues:** three-column text card: "Directly transfers / Partially transfers / Real gap"
