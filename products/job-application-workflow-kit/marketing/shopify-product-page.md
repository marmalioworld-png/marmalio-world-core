## Hero copy

Most "AI resume prompts" give you one clever prompt and leave you to figure out the rest. Marmalio Apply is different: a complete, step-by-step workflow that takes you from a job posting and your real experience all the way to a tailored resume, cover letter, LinkedIn profile, interview prep, and negotiation script — built entirely from what you actually did, never from what sounds good.

## What's inside

- **Start Here Guide** — the full system explained, plus a 30-minute quick-start path if you're on a deadline.
- **Market Conventions guide** — what genuinely differs between the USA, UK, Canada, and Australia (terminology, length, personal information norms, spelling, currency) so you're not applying US-only rules in a UK or Australian application.
- **9-prompt library** — Job Posting Analysis, Experience Inventory, Resume Bullet Rewrite, Resume Tailoring, Cover Letter, LinkedIn Headline & Summary, STAR Interview Prep, Salary Negotiation, Career-Change Narrative. Every prompt includes required inputs, expected output, a truth check, and a refinement step.
- **Two one-column resume templates** (experience-forward and skills-forward), built with a plain, ATS-conscious structure.
- **Cover letter template.**
- **Four step-by-step workflows** for LinkedIn optimization, interview preparation, salary negotiation, and career changes.
- **Truth & Quality Checklist** — run every output through this before you send it anywhere.
- **Three complete worked examples** (mid-career professional, career changer, recent graduate) showing the entire system in action.

## Who this is for

- You're applying to real, specific job postings and want your materials genuinely tailored to each one, not generic.
- You already have real experience, education, or projects — this system organizes and presents what you've actually done.
- You use ChatGPT, Claude, or a similar AI assistant and want a repeatable process instead of guessing at prompts.
- You're in the USA, UK, Canada, or Australia — the included Market Conventions guide covers what differs between "resume" and "CV" markets (length, personal information norms, spelling, and currency for negotiation), so nothing here assumes only one country's rules.
