# Shopify FAQ Copy

**Q: Does this guarantee me an interview or a job offer?**
No. No product can honestly guarantee that — hiring decisions depend on many factors outside any document's control. This system helps you present your real experience clearly and consistently; it doesn't control how any employer responds.

**Q: Is this just a list of ChatGPT prompts?**
No. It's a complete workflow: 9 prompts used in a specific order, each producing an output the next step depends on, plus two resume templates, a cover letter template, four detailed workflows (LinkedIn, interview prep, negotiation, career change), a Truth & Quality Checklist, and three full worked examples. The prompts are one part of a larger system, not the whole product.

**Q: Will this make up things I haven't actually done?**
No — and this is the core design principle of the entire system. Every prompt explicitly instructs the AI to use only the information you provide and to flag gaps instead of guessing. The included Truth & Quality Checklist exists specifically to catch anything that slips through. See the Limitations & Safe Use section for the full detail.

**Q: Does this "beat" ATS software?**
We don't claim that, because it isn't a claim anyone can honestly make — ATS systems vary between employers and change over time. What this system produces is a plain, single-column, consistently-structured document, which is good, honest formatting practice. We call this "ATS-conscious," not "ATS-proof" or "ATS-ready."

**Q: Do I need a paid ChatGPT or Claude subscription?**
No. The prompts work with free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude, or similar assistants. Paid tiers may produce somewhat richer output, but nothing in this system requires one.

**Q: What exact files do I get?**
A ZIP file containing: the Start Here Guide (PDF), the complete guide with all prompts, workflows, examples, and checklists (PDF), a copy-paste prompt sheet, two resume templates (DOCX), and a cover letter template (DOCX). See the packaging manifest for the exact file list.

**Q: Can I use this for a career change?**
Yes — there's a dedicated Career-Change Workflow and prompt built specifically to create an honest transferable-skills narrative, rather than implying direct experience you don't have.

**Q: What if I'm a recent graduate with limited work experience?**
The system includes a skills-forward resume template and a worked example specifically for a recent graduate with a single internship and a class project — see the included examples.

**Q: Is this available in languages other than English?**
Not yet. The initial release is English, built for the USA, UK, Canada, and Australia — the resume/CV guidance, terminology, and examples are written to work across all four (see the included Market Conventions guide). Polish, German, Spanish, and French versions are planned as separate, fully localized releases, not machine translations, once the English version is live.

**Q: Does the price shown change based on my country?**
Prices display in your local currency automatically at checkout wherever Shopify's currency conversion is enabled for your store. The list price is set in USD.
