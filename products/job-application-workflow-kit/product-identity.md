Owner-approved for production. Formal trademark clearance is still required before public launch — see the naming caveat in `README.md`. Never use "CareerBoost AI" or "ApplyFlow."

## Internal product code

`job-application-workflow-kit` — directory and file-path code only. Never shown to a customer.

## Tagline

Build truthful, role-aligned application materials with a clear, ATS-conscious workflow.

## One-sentence value proposition

Marmalio Apply turns one real job posting and your real experience into a tailored resume, cover letter, LinkedIn profile, and interview preparation — through a complete nine-step guided workflow, not a list of prompts, and never by inventing anything you haven't actually done.

## Target customer

A job seeker in the USA, Canada, UK, Australia, or another English-speaking market who is applying to a specific, real job posting, already has real work experience, education, or projects to draw on, and uses ChatGPT, Claude, or a similar AI assistant but wants a structured, repeatable process instead of guessing at one-off prompts.

## Premium-quality principles

- Premium means quality, completeness, and professional execution — not an inflated price.
- Complete guided workflow, not a prompt collection: every step depends on the output of the one before it.
- Every prompt defines required inputs, expected output, a truth check, and a refinement step — no exceptions.
- Never invents a candidate's experience, qualifications, achievements, or results — every prompt instructs the AI to use only what the candidate actually supplied and flag gaps instead of guessing.
- No single format is presented as correct for every country — USA/Canada, UK, Australia, and general international guidance are kept clearly distinct.
- No claim appears anywhere that a professional, honest business wouldn't stand behind in every target market.

## Approved claims

- "A complete, step-by-step workflow" / "a complete guided workflow, not a prompt pack."
- "Build truthful, role-aligned application materials with a clear, ATS-conscious workflow" (the tagline, used verbatim).
- "ATS-conscious structure" — describing plain, single-column, standard-heading formatting as good practice.
- "Built entirely from what you actually did" / "never invents your experience."
- "Nine connected steps" / "a repeatable process."

## Prohibited claims

- "Beat ATS," "ATS-ready," "ATS-proof," or any wording implying guaranteed applicant-tracking-system success.
- "Land interviews," "guaranteed interview," "guaranteed job," or any promise of a hiring outcome.
- Any claim of legal, tax, immigration, or career-counseling advice.
- Any claim that the system verifies, fact-checks, or guarantees the accuracy of information the candidate provides.
- Any claim implying certification, compliance, or an official endorsement of any kind.
