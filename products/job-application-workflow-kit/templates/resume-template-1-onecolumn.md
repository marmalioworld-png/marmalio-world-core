# Resume Template 1 — One-Column, Experience-Forward

**Design intent for DOCX production:** single column, no tables, no text boxes, no graphics or icons, standard heading styles (Heading 1/2 in Word), one plain font throughout (e.g., Calibri, Arial, or Georgia, 10.5–11pt body / 14–16pt name), generous white space, standard section order top to bottom. This structure is described as **ATS-conscious** — a plain, linearly-parseable layout — not claimed as "ATS-ready" or "guaranteed to pass" any system.

Use this variant when the candidate's work history is the strongest selling point (most candidates, most roles).

**Market note:** this structure is deliberately generic across the USA, UK, Canada, and Australia — no photo field, no date-of-birth field, `[City, State/Country]` instead of a full postal address, and written-out dates (`Jan 2021 – Present`) instead of numeric ones. This is not an accident; see `00b-market-conventions.md` before adding anything this template leaves out, or before adjusting the one-page (US/Canada) vs. two-page (UK) vs. two-to-three-page (Australia) length guidance below.

## Formatting notes for whoever builds the final DOCX/PDF in Canva or Google Docs

- Keep section headers in a consistent style (bold, small caps, or a thin rule underneath — pick one, use it everywhere).
- No columns, no tables for layout, no headers/footers containing information the file needs to be read without (some ATS parsers drop header/footer content).
- One page for under ~8 years of experience, two pages maximum otherwise, for USA/Canada. For UK, plan for 2 pages as the standard. For Australia, 2–3 pages is commonly accepted. See `00b-market-conventions.md`.
- Save/export as both `.docx` (editable) and `.pdf` (submission-ready) — see `packaging-manifest.md`.
