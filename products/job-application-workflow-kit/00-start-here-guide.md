# Start Here: Marmalio Apply — Global Job Application Workflow Kit

## What this is

This is a complete, step-by-step system for turning one real job posting and your real experience into a tailored resume, cover letter, LinkedIn profile update, interview preparation, and — if you need it — a salary negotiation script and a career-change narrative.

It is not a list of prompts to skim. It is a workflow: each step produces an output that the next step depends on. Followed in order, it takes you from "I found a job posting" to "I have a complete, honest application package for this specific role."

## Who this is for

- You are applying to a specific job posting right now, not writing a generic resume for no particular role.
- You already have real work experience, education, or projects to draw on — this system organizes and presents what you've actually done. It does not invent a career for you.
- You use ChatGPT, Claude, or a similar AI assistant, and want a structured way to get consistently good output instead of guessing at prompts each time.
- You're applying in the USA, Canada, UK, Australia, or another English-speaking market. This kit uses "resume" as its working term throughout — if your market calls it a "CV" (UK, Australia, and most markets outside the US/Canada), that's the same document; see `00b-market-conventions.md` for the full breakdown of what else differs by market (length, personal information norms, spelling, currency for negotiation), including guidance for markets beyond the four with detailed conventions.

## The one rule that matters most

**Nothing in this system invents your experience, qualifications, achievements, or results.**

Every prompt in this kit is written so the AI only reorganizes, clarifies, and tailors information you provide yourself. If a prompt's output ever states something you didn't tell it — a number, a title, an outcome, a skill — that is a mistake to correct, not a feature to keep. The `quality/truth-and-quality-checklist.md` file exists specifically to catch this before you send anything to an employer. Use it every time.

## Honest positioning

Marmalio Apply helps you **build truthful, role-aligned application materials with a clear, ATS-conscious workflow.** It does not promise to "beat" applicant tracking systems, does not guarantee interviews, and does not guarantee a job offer. What it does is remove the guesswork from turning your real background into materials that are clear, consistent, and matched to the role you're applying for. See `quality/limitations-and-safe-use.md` for the full, honest list of what this system does not do.

## Before you start: check your market's conventions

Read `00b-market-conventions.md` first if you're outside the USA — it covers terminology (resume vs. CV), expected length, what personal information to include or omit, address format, date format, spelling, and which local salary-research sources to use for Step 8. The templates and prompts in this kit default to conventions that are safe across all four launch markets (USA, UK, Canada, Australia); that file tells you where those defaults come from and when to adjust them.

## The workflow at a glance

1. **Job Posting Analysis** (`prompts/01-job-posting-analysis.md`) — understand exactly what the role is asking for, without assuming you already match it.
2. **Experience Inventory** (`prompts/02-experience-inventory.md`) — the foundation step. You answer structured questions about your real background; this becomes the single source of truth every later step draws from.
3. **Resume Bullet Rewrite** (`prompts/03-resume-bullet-rewrite.md`) — turn your raw inventory into clear, strong resume bullets, with nothing added.
4. **Resume Tailoring** (`prompts/04-resume-tailoring.md`) — select and order those bullets to match the specific posting from Step 1.
5. **Cover Letter** (`prompts/05-cover-letter.md`) — a letter grounded only in what Steps 1–2 established.
6. **LinkedIn Optimization** (`workflows/linkedin-optimization-workflow.md`) — bring your profile in line with the same, truthful positioning.
7. **STAR Interview Preparation** (`workflows/star-interview-prep-workflow.md`) — turn your real stories into structured interview answers.
8. **Salary Negotiation Preparation** (`workflows/salary-negotiation-workflow.md`) — optional, once you have an offer or are approaching that stage.
9. **Career-Change Workflow** (`workflows/career-change-workflow.md`) — optional, if you're moving into a new field and need a transferable-skills narrative instead of a direct-experience one.

## How to use a prompt

Every file in `prompts/` follows the same format:

- **Purpose** — what this step does and why it exists.
- **Required Inputs** — what you need to have ready before you paste the prompt. Don't skip this; incomplete inputs are the most common cause of weak output.
- **The Prompt** — paste this into ChatGPT, Claude, or your assistant of choice, filling in the bracketed placeholders with your real information.
- **Expected Output** — what a good response looks like, so you can tell if the AI followed the instructions.
- **Truth Check** — a specific question to ask about the output before you trust it.
- **Refinement Step** — what to paste back in if the output needs correcting.

## Quick-start path (if you only have 30 minutes)

If you're short on time before a deadline, the minimum useful path is: Step 1 (Job Posting Analysis) → Step 2 (Experience Inventory, focused only on the most relevant 2–3 roles) → Step 4 (Resume Tailoring, using one of the templates in `templates/`) → a fast pass through `quality/truth-and-quality-checklist.md`. Add the remaining steps as time allows.

## What you'll produce

By the end, you will have: a tailored resume (from one of the two one-column templates), a tailored cover letter, updated LinkedIn headline and About section, a set of STAR-format interview answers specific to this role, and — if relevant — negotiation talking points and a career-change narrative. Every one of these is built from your own words, checked against the Truth & Quality Checklist before use.
