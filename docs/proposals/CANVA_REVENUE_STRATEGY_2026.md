This repository document mirrors the substance of the uploaded proposal so repository agents can review it durably.

## 1. Goal

Build Canva into a production layer for digital products and services that can quickly test demand and then scale winning categories across products, niches and languages. Canva is a production layer, not the only sales channel.

## 2. Revenue models proposed

### A. Canva Creators

Apply to Canva's official Template Creator program. If accepted, publish original templates in Canva's marketplace and earn royalties based on usage/popularity.

### B. Sell original Canva templates externally

Examples:
- social media kits;
- media kits;
- pitch decks;
- CV/resume kits;
- menus;
- price lists;
- invitations;
- workbooks.

### C. Finished digital products

Examples:
- PDFs;
- checklists;
- planners;
- workbooks;
- ebooks;
- printables;
- worksheets;
- resources for small businesses and creators.

### D. Productized services

Examples:
- 30-post social pack for a salon;
- restaurant menu pack;
- real-estate social kit;
- creator media kit.

These can carry a higher ticket than a single template.

### E. B2B niche packs

Vertical packs for:
- beauty;
- fitness;
- gastronomy;
- real estate;
- coaching;
- local services;
- creator economy.

### F. Localization

Localize proven winners into additional languages/markets after validation rather than translating the full catalog immediately.

## 3. Original MVP portfolio proposal

1. Social Media Business Kit — beauty / gastro / fitness / real estate — fast B2B demand test.
2. Creator / UGC Media Kit — 3 styles + 2 formats — creator economy.
3. CV + Cover Letter Kit — DE / EN / PL — evergreen.
4. Small Business Price List + Menu — salon / barber / cafe / services — local-business use case.
5. Lead Magnet / Workbook Kit — coach / consultant / creator — B2B sale.
6. Pitch Deck / Proposal Kit — agency / freelancer / startup — higher ticket.
7. Wedding / Event Pack — invite / menu / seating / signage bundle.
8. Printable Planner Pack — weekly / budget / goals — mass-market product.

## 4. Multi-channel sales strategy

Layer 1 — Canva marketplace through Canva Creators after acceptance.

Layer 2 — external marketplaces intended for digital products/design assets.

Layer 3 — own checkout/storefront for control of margin, bundles and upsells, with platform choice subject to separate tax/VAT/consumer-law/integration review.

Proposal principle: do not build a huge store first. Start with 10–20 strong offers, measure clicks/sales, then multiply winners.

## 5. Proposed Canva Content Factory workflow

1. RESEARCH — niche, problem, keywords, style, format.
2. SPEC — target audience, use case, page count, variants, languages, license.
3. DESIGN SYSTEM — 2–4 base styles, typography, spacing, components, quality rules.
4. PRODUCTION — original master template in Canva.
5. QA — mobile/desktop/print readability, editability, no placeholder errors, rights review.
6. PACKAGING — mockups, thumbnails, usage instructions, delivery file/template link, FAQ.
7. LISTING — title, description, benefits, keywords, sales graphics.
8. LAUNCH TEST — small batch, no paid ads without HUMAN APPROVAL.
9. DATA LOOP — sales, CTR, conversion, refunds, customer questions.
10. SCALE — bundle, niche variant, language, format, upsell; retire weak products.

## 7. Licensing / compliance points from the proposal

- Sold design must be original; do not resell standalone Canva content or existing Canva templates as-is.
- Canva permits use in certain digital/physical products, but licensing conditions apply.
- Editable templates using Canva Pro Content require particular license-safe handling; the proposal highlights sale via Canva template links rather than redistributing Pro Content as standalone files.
- Canva Creators requires application/acceptance.
- AI can assist ideation/efficiency, but obvious AI-generated or insufficiently original Creator submissions may be rejected.
- Before scaled publication, review Canva license terms, marketplace rules, VAT/tax, consumer law, refunds, trademark/copyright and Germany Business Setup.

## 8. Economics

Do not treat revenue numbers as forecasts.

Test three value levels:
- low-ticket single product;
- mid-ticket bundle;
- high-ticket productized service.

Early metrics:
- production time per listing;
- conversion rate;
- revenue per listing;
- refund/problem rate;
- ability to create variants without quality loss.

## 9. Original 30-day proposal

- Days 1–3: compliance review + 3 niches + product standard + 2 design systems.
- Days 4–7: 10 MVP products + mockups + listing pack + QA.
- Week 2: publish after HUMAN APPROVAL; gather data; apply to Canva Creators if portfolio is ready.
- Week 3: 20–30 variants only from first demand signals; first bundles.
- Week 4: kill/keep/scale review; localize winners; productized-service test; automation backlog.

## 10. ChatGPT review note — APPROVE WITH CHANGES

The strategy fits Architecture v2.2, but before implementation the following adjustments are recommended for independent Codex review:

1. Canva should be treated primarily as part of the existing **Digital Product Factory + Content Factory + B2B Productized Services**, not as a completely separate business silo.
2. Do not make Canva Creators the main first-revenue path; external/owned sales of original templates and finished digital products can be tested in parallel while the Creator application is pending.
3. Replace the aggressive `10 MVP products in days 4–7` target with a quality-first sequence: **3–5 premium master products -> 10–15 high-quality offers/variants -> data -> scale winners**.
4. Keep the proposal's `10–20 offers / 3 niches before mass automation` rule.
5. Preserve strict licensing evidence for Canva assets, fonts, stock, templates and AI-assisted work.
6. Optimize for reusable premium master assets that can become: template, bundle, localized variant, B2B service pack, social asset and content topic.
