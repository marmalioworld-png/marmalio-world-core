# Marmalio World — Germany Business Setup 2026

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: working recommendation; tax elections require confirmation before filing
Purpose: define the safest low-overhead legal/tax starting structure for Marmalio World in Germany.

This document is operational research, not individual tax/legal advice.

## 1. Recommended starting legal form

### Working recommendation

Start Marmalio World as an **Einzelunternehmen (sole proprietorship) with Gewerbeanmeldung**, initially run as a small commercial business rather than forming a UG/GmbH immediately.

Reasoning:

- the planned activity is a mixed commercial business: digital products, creator/media monetization, affiliate income, Amazon KDP/Merch, later e-commerce/dropshipping;
- Munich treats a profit-oriented commercial activity as a Gewerbe and requires registration before or at commencement;
- a sole proprietorship is the simplest and cheapest launch form;
- no notarial formation or commercial-register entry is required for an ordinary small sole proprietorship;
- simplified profit determination (typically EÜR while eligible) and lower fixed administration are appropriate for validation-stage revenue;
- sole proprietors benefit from the EUR 24,500 trade-tax allowance; corporations do not.

### Important terminology

Do not confuse these terms:

- `Einzelunternehmen` = legal/business form.
- `Kleingewerbe` = colloquial/small-business commercial status; not a separate legal form.
- `Nebengewerbe` = business operated as a side activity; not a separate legal form.
- `Kleinunternehmerregelung` = VAT treatment under § 19 UStG; not a legal form and not an income-tax exemption.

### Brand name

`Marmalio World` may be used as a business/brand designation, but the legal identity of the sole proprietor must remain correctly visible on invoices, legal notices and contracts where required.

## 2. When registration becomes mandatory

Munich states that a commercial business registration must be filed **in good time before, or at the latest when, the commercial activity begins**. Actual profit is not required; the intention to make profit is enough.

For Marmalio World, the conservative trigger is:

**Complete Gewerbeanmeldung before the first public revenue-oriented launch.**

Do not wait for the first payout or first customer if the activity is already being operated publicly with a profit motive.

Activities that may remain in preparation before that point include internal product drafting, private research, repository development and closed/test-mode checkout tests with no real sales. Once products are publicly offered, affiliate links are used commercially, monetized creator activity is operated as a business, or real sales/orders are accepted, the business should already be registered.

Official Munich source:
https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/gewerbeamt/10164604/

Munich currently states a Gewerbeanmeldung fee range of approximately EUR 50–60 depending on legal form.

## 3. Tax registration deadline

After business commencement, the tax-registration information (`Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung`) must be transmitted electronically to the Finanzamt **within one month** under § 138 AO.

Sources:
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__138.html
https://www.faq.elster.de/elsterweb/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_fseeun_202301

Operational rule: prepare ELSTER before launch instead of waiting until after the first sales.

## 4. Initial tax treatment

### Income tax

A sole proprietor pays income tax on **profit**, not turnover. Business expenses reduce taxable business profit when deductible.

The 2026 German basic personal allowance (`Grundfreibetrag`) is EUR 12,348, but this applies to overall taxable income, not as an additional tax-free allowance reserved only for the business. If the owner has other taxable income, business profit is generally added to the overall taxable income.

Source:
https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Monatsberichte/Ausgabe/2026/02/Inhalte/Kapitel-2-Analysen/2-5-wichtigste-steuerliche-aenderungen-2026-pdf.pdf

Operational cash rule: until an individual tax estimate is calculated, reserve a meaningful portion of business profit for tax rather than treating all cash received as spendable. IHK München gives a practical rule of thumb of roughly 30% of profit for side-business tax reserves, but the correct percentage depends on the owner's total taxable income and circumstances.

### Trade tax (Gewerbesteuer)

For an Einzelunternehmen/person partnership, the statutory trade-tax allowance is **EUR 24,500 of Gewerbeertrag**. Only the amount above the allowance enters the trade-tax calculation.

Sources:
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewstg/__11.html
https://www.ihk-muenchen.de/recht-gewerbesteuer/

Munich's 2026 Gewerbesteuer Hebesatz is **490%**.

Source:
https://stadt.muenchen.de/infos/hebesaetze-gewerbesteuer-grundsteuer.html

For persons/partnerships, trade tax can be credited against income tax within statutory limits; because Munich's rate is above roughly 400%, the credit may not eliminate the full Munich trade-tax burden once the allowance is exceeded.

### VAT / Kleinunternehmerregelung

Since 2025 the § 19 UStG limits are:

- previous-year total turnover: max EUR 25,000;
- current-year total turnover: max EUR 100,000.

For a **new business in its founding year**, IHK München states that the relevant founding-year threshold is **EUR 25,000 actual turnover**, without annualizing a partial year. Once the founding-year threshold is exceeded, the excess-triggering turnover is no longer covered by the exemption.

Sources:
https://www.ihk-muenchen.de/ratgeber/steuern/steuerarten/umsatzsteuer/kleinunternehmerregelung/
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__19.html

### Recommendation on Kleinunternehmer status

Do **not** select or reject Kleinunternehmer status automatically.

Working decision rule before filing ELSTER:

- If realistic actual 2026 Marmalio World turnover is expected to stay below EUR 25,000 and input-VAT-bearing startup costs are modest, Kleinunternehmer treatment may reduce VAT administration and can be practical during validation.
- If there is a credible chance that 2026 turnover will exceed EUR 25,000 quickly, or material VAT-bearing investments are planned, regular VAT taxation may be operationally cleaner than crossing the founding-year threshold shortly after launch.
- Lemon Squeezy Merchant-of-Record sales, Amazon/platform payouts, affiliate income and purchases of foreign SaaS/services require transaction-specific VAT/accounting treatment; Kleinunternehmer status does not make every VAT issue disappear.

`PROFESSIONAL CHECK`: confirm the Kleinunternehmer election and cross-border platform treatment with a German Steuerberater before filing the questionnaire.

## 5. Why not UG/GmbH at launch

### UG (haftungsbeschränkt)

Pros:
- limited-liability corporate vehicle after registration;
- can start with low nominal capital.

Cons for the current validation stage:
- notarial formation + commercial-register process;
- separate corporate accounting/annual accounts and disclosure duties;
- corporate tax + trade tax at company level and additional tax consequences when profit is distributed;
- no EUR 24,500 Gewerbesteuer allowance for capital companies;
- 25% of annual surplus must generally be retained in the statutory reserve until the capital target is reached.

IHK source:
https://www.ihk-muenchen.de/ratgeber/recht/gesellschaftsrecht/ug-gruenden/

### GmbH

Not recommended at validation stage due to EUR 25,000 nominal share capital, higher formation/administration burden and full corporate accounting requirements.

IHK source:
https://www.ihk-muenchen.de/ratgeber/recht/gesellschaftsrecht/gmbh-gruenden/

### Revisit trigger for limited-liability entity

Reassess UG/GmbH when one or more of the following becomes true:

- meaningful and stable recurring profit exists;
- physical products/dropshipping materially increase product-liability/contract risk;
- employees/contractors or larger commercial contracts are added;
- substantial retained earnings are kept in the business;
- external investment/partners are planned;
- a tax adviser identifies a measurable tax/structural advantage that exceeds the added accounting/admin cost.

## 6. IHK costs

Commercial businesses normally become IHK members. IHK München states:

- non-HR-registered small businesses can be contribution-exempt if annual profit/earnings do not exceed EUR 5,200;
- qualifying first-time founders who are natural persons, not registered in the Handelsregister and have annual earnings not above EUR 25,000 can be contribution-exempt for the first two years and pay only the basic contribution in years 3–4 under the stated conditions.

Source:
https://www.ihk-muenchen.de/ueber-uns/beitrag/

## 7. Immediate execution plan

Before first public Marmalio revenue launch:

1. Confirm exact business start date.
2. Use the legal form `Einzelunternehmen` unless professional review produces a strong reason to choose differently.
3. Draft a broad but accurate Gewerbe activity description covering digital products, online content/media monetization, affiliate marketing, print-on-demand and later e-commerce without implying regulated financial/legal/medical services.
4. Submit Munich Gewerbeanmeldung before or at activity start.
5. Ensure ELSTER access is ready.
6. Within one month, submit `Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung`.
7. Before filing the VAT section, confirm Kleinunternehmer vs regular VAT with a German Steuerberater using a 2026 turnover forecast and the actual platform mix.
8. Set up bookkeeping from day one: payouts, platform fees, refunds, business expenses, invoices/statements and tax reserve.
9. Determine the competent Berufsgenossenschaft / statutory accident-insurance registration requirement for the actual activity.
10. Only then switch test commerce into real public sales after the other Marmalio compliance gates are satisfied.

## 8. Current project recommendation

**START:** Einzelunternehmen + Gewerbeanmeldung.

**VAT:** likely Kleinunternehmer candidate only if the 2026 founding-year turnover forecast remains under EUR 25,000; final election requires explicit confirmation before ELSTER filing because Marmalio World is intentionally designed to scale quickly and uses international platforms.

**LATER:** reassess UG/GmbH after proven revenue or when physical-commerce liability makes limited liability materially valuable.
