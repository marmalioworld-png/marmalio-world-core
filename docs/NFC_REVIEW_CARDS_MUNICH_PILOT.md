# Marmalio World — NFC Review Cards Munich Pilot

Date: 2026-08-27
Status: APPROVED FOR RESEARCH / WEEKEND REVIEW; NOT YET APPROVED FOR PURCHASE, OUTREACH OR PUBLIC SALE
Role: Local Cashflow Pilot / B2B Munich

## 1. Purpose

Test a low-cost local B2B offer that may generate cashflow faster than purely digital channels while the main Marmalio World Product Factory and Media Factory continue in parallel.

Offer concept:
- premium NFC + QR review card / stand;
- configured to open the client's legitimate Google review link;
- client branding and simple premium design;
- optional on-site setup/installation.

This is not a new core brand direction. It is a bounded local revenue experiment.

## 2. Initial target customers

Prioritize owner-operated businesses where the decision maker can often be reached locally:
- barbers / hairdressers;
- beauty / nail salons;
- restaurants, cafes and small food businesses;
- auto detailing / workshops;
- small hotels / guesthouses;
- gyms / fitness studios;
- small local retail/service businesses.

Large chains are not a priority for the pilot because procurement/branding decisions are usually centralized.

## 3. Validation design

Do NOT buy a large stock.

Preferred validation sequence:
1. Research legal/platform/supplier requirements.
2. Build one premium working demo plus only a few spare tags/cards if needed.
3. Prepare 2–3 visual concepts in Canva.
4. Prepare a 30–60 second in-person demo/sales explanation.
5. Select a small Munich test cluster and 30–50 owner-operated businesses.
6. Test offers and record every YES / NO / objection.
7. Require several real paying businesses and confirmed margin before scaling.

No outreach or purchase is authorized by this document alone.

## 4. Pricing hypothesis for weekend review

Do not lock prices until competitor and willingness-to-pay research is complete.

Working hypothesis to test:
- Basic: around EUR 79;
- Premium: around EUR 119;
- Business / multi-unit: around EUR 199–299 depending on quantity, design and installation.

Reason: Marmalio should sell a configured premium service/result, not compete only on the raw cost of an NFC card.

All prices are hypotheses, not published offers.

## 5. Success / stop criteria

Pilot success target:
- 3–5 real paying businesses;
- positive gross margin after card/stand/print/travel/time costs;
- evidence that the offer can be explained and demonstrated quickly;
- no material Google-policy, consumer, product-safety, privacy, trademark or tax blocker.

Stop / redesign if:
- 30–50 qualified conversations produce insufficient paid demand;
- realistic margin is too low after labor/travel/customization;
- Google policy or legal constraints make the core offer unattractive;
- supplier/product-quality risk cannot be controlled cheaply.

## 6. Google review integrity rules

The product may only make it easier to reach a legitimate review flow.

Never design or sell a workflow that:
- rewards/incentivizes reviews;
- gives discounts/free products in exchange for reviews;
- suppresses negative reviews;
- routes only satisfied customers to Google;
- asks for fabricated or biased reviews;
- promises a specific star rating or review result.

Weekend research must verify current Google Business Profile / Maps review policies directly from current first-party Google sources before outreach.

## 7. Compliance research required before launch

Before purchase/outreach/public sale, verify at minimum:
- German business/tax fit with the existing Marmalio Einzelunternehmen/Gewerbe plan;
- whether the activity description for Gewerbeanmeldung covers this local physical/service offer;
- Google review solicitation policy;
- German/EU unfair-commercial-practice / advertising issues around review generation;
- GDPR/privacy implications of QR/NFC landing or tracking links;
- product-safety / GPSR obligations for physical cards/stands sold to businesses;
- manufacturer/importer/responsible-person information where applicable;
- packaging obligations (VerpackG/LUCID) if Marmalio places packaged goods on the German market/shipping is used;
- electrical/CE/WEEE/battery obligations only if any chosen product actually falls into those regimes;
- trademark/logo permission for customized client branding;
- supplier quality and commercial-use terms;
- invoice/bookkeeping treatment;
- liability and return/replacement terms for B2B sales;
- any local Munich requirements relevant to installation or on-site sales activity.

Do not assume NFC hardware is exempt from every product-compliance rule simply because it is cheap/passive.
