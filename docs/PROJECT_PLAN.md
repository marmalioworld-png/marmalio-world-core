# Marmalio World — Project Plan

## Mission

Build a global ecosystem of premium digital products, media assets, Amazon products, affiliate revenue, and later physical commerce under the Marmalio World brand — one umbrella ecosystem, not unrelated projects.

Current approved architecture: `docs/ARCHITECTURE_V2_2_MULTI_REVENUE.md`.
Mandatory launch/compliance rules: `docs/COMPLIANCE_GATES_2026.md`.

## Revenue-first execution directive

Marmalio World exists to create durable revenue-producing assets. Infrastructure, orchestration and automation support that goal; they are not the product.

- Prioritize products, listings, distribution, media, Amazon adaptations, affiliate opportunities and sales.
- Do not stop product creation after one validation product. Build a small premium portfolio in sequence.
- Keep technical/infrastructure work constrained unless it directly supports a named revenue asset.
- Every product sprint ends with final customer files, pricing, listing copy, cover/mockups, delivery setup, QA and launch materials.
- Human approval remains required before publishing, spending, paid advertising, account creation, external outreach, production deployment, destructive actions or major architectural changes.

## Multi-revenue model

### 1. Digital Product Factory — highest priority

Initial portfolio:

- `MW-DIG-000001` — The AI Productive Prompt Pack — target EUR 10
- `MW-DIG-000002` — next validated digital product
- `MW-DIG-000003`
- `MW-DIG-000004`
- `MW-DIG-000005`

Approved families: ebooks, guides, workbooks, templates, checklists, prompt packs and document packs. Courses later.

Validation-phase digital commerce: **Lemon Squeezy**.
Shopify is deferred for the validation digital-product sprint and returns when physical products/dropshipping or another approved storefront need makes it materially useful.

### 2. Media / Audience Factory

Start early and run in parallel with product creation.

Core channels:

- YouTube long-form
- YouTube Shorts
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels

Two content classes:

- `SALES_CONTENT` — useful content that may lead to Marmalio products, affiliate offers or other approved offers.
- `MEDIA_ASSET` — content built primarily for audience growth and platform monetization through views, subscriptions, ads, sponsorship or affiliate revenue.

Kids/family content must use a separate channel/lane and follow the dedicated child-safety and platform rules in the compliance gates.

### 3. Amazon Factory

- Amazon KDP: adapt suitable Marmalio products into ebooks, workbooks and print formats.
- Amazon Merch on Demand: original/licensed design collections, beginning with quality batches and scaling only proven themes.

### 4. Affiliate Engine

Use relevant affiliate programs as an additional monetization layer for useful content, with clear disclosure and program-policy compliance.

### 5. Physical Commerce / Dropshipping

Planned, not immediate priority.

Before launch: verify supplier, sample quality, delivery time, returns, full margin, customer-support burden, product-safety obligations, labeling and responsible economic operator requirements where applicable.

### 6. A11y Evidence

A11y Evidence is part of the opportunity portfolio but remains on HOLD for public deployment/outreach until the conditions in `docs/A11Y_EVIDENCE_G1_STATUS.md` are satisfied.

## Revenue gate

`R10 = 10 real paid customer orders across the live Marmalio digital-product portfolio.`

Before R10:

- keep creating/publishing approved premium products;
- build audience/content;
- prepare/publish approved Amazon assets;
- learn Make if useful, but do not make it a critical production dependency.

After R10:

- identify winners;
- reassess Make Core and sanitized analytics;
- automate only repeated workflows with proven value;
- increase production around winners rather than blindly increasing volume.

## Operating model

**Human → ChatGPT → repository spec/decision → Claude Code / explicitly assigned Codex → specialist agents → QA → human approval**

- Human: intent, priorities, final approval.
- ChatGPT: strategic manager / architect.
- Claude Code: primary implementation/execution layer.
- Codex: independent repo cross-reviewer / implementation when explicitly assigned.
- Specialist roles: Research, Product Builder, Localization, Compliance, QA.
- QA target: at least 85/100 before publication unless human owner explicitly overrides.

Durable AI-to-AI handoff happens through repository files/specs/PRs, not assumptions about chat memory.

## Trend / premium production principle

Every product/content batch follows:

research current demand → score opportunity → create premium original asset → platform-native packaging → factual/language/design/IP/compliance QA → human approval where required → publish → measure → kill weak patterns → scale winners.

Do not treat viral screenshots, influencer revenue claims or unverified social content as market evidence without current verification.

## Compliance

No revenue engine bypasses `docs/COMPLIANCE_GATES_2026.md`.

The project must create auditable evidence for business/tax setup, consumer rights, privacy/GDPR, IP/licensing, AI disclosures, platform policies, child-safety rules and physical-product safety before the relevant launch.

## Business objective

Build a sustainable online business generating 10,000+ EUR net per month through multiple complementary revenue engines, while protecting account longevity, legal compliance, customer trust and premium quality.
