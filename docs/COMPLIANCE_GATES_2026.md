# Marmalio World — Compliance Gates 2026

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: mandatory launch checklist
Purpose: reduce legal, tax, consumer-protection, privacy, IP and platform risk before each revenue engine goes live.

This is an operational checklist, not legal or tax advice. Where this file marks `PROFESSIONAL CHECK`, obtain qualified German/EU professional confirmation before launch or scale.

## Gate 1 — Website / seller identity / legal pages

Before a Marmalio-controlled website/storefront is publicly used for commercial purposes:

- provide legally sufficient provider/seller identification (`Impressum`) where required;
- provide a privacy notice (`Datenschutzerklärung`) covering actual tools, forms, analytics, embeds and processors;
- implement cookie/consent controls where non-essential cookies/tracking require consent;
- do not copy generic legal templates without matching them to the real stack;
- ensure commercial contact details are current and monitored;
- record which third-party platforms are controllers/processors/independent sellers/Merchant of Record.

## Gate 2 — Digital product consumer rights

For each paid digital download:

- accurately describe what the buyer receives, file format, language, compatibility and delivery method;
- show total price and material conditions before purchase;
- do not use fake scarcity, fake reviews, fake countdowns or misleading claims;
- confirm the platform checkout correctly handles any required consumer information;
- where immediate digital delivery affects the statutory withdrawal right, ensure the checkout obtains the required express consent/acknowledgement where applicable;
- provide a clear support/refund route consistent with the commerce platform's terms and applicable consumer law;
- retain the version of product/listing/terms that was live at purchase time where practical.

Lemon Squeezy's Merchant-of-Record role may handle transaction tax/VAT and parts of checkout compliance, but it does not remove Marmalio World's own German income-tax, advertising, IP, product-description, privacy or business-registration obligations.

## Gate 3 — Privacy / GDPR / data minimization

Global rule:

- collect only data needed for a stated purpose;
- customer PII stays in approved commerce/correspondence systems;
- do not copy customer names, emails, addresses or payment information into GitHub, Notion, Drive, prompts, generic AI chats, Make blueprints or analytics logs;
- document retention periods and deletion paths;
- execute DPAs / processor terms where required;
- use separate credentials and least-privilege access;
- never commit secrets.

For marketing email/newsletters:

- use a compliant opt-in model for markets where consent is required;
- maintain suppression/unsubscribe handling;
- do not turn purchased/scraped lists into automated outreach without a market-specific legal review.

## Gate 4 — Copyright, trademarks, personality rights and licenses

Every product, video, thumbnail, design, book and ad must pass an IP check.

- Use original content or content with documented commercial rights.
- Keep license/source evidence for stock media, music, sound effects, fonts, templates, footage and datasets.
- Do not use copyrighted characters, movie/game/anime franchises, celebrity images, logos or protected brands as merchandise/content bait without rights.
- Do not imitate a living creator's voice/likeness in a deceptive way.
- Check product/brand names for obvious trademark conflicts before scaling a line.
- AI generation does not guarantee non-infringement; outputs still require review.

## Gate 5 — YouTube

Before publishing:

- comply with Community Guidelines, copyright and monetization policies;
- use licensed/original music, footage and imagery;
- correctly classify `Made for Kids` where applicable;
- understand that Made-for-Kids content has restricted features and personalized advertising limitations;
- avoid repetitive, mass-produced, low-value or reused content intended only to game monetization;
- disclose altered/synthetic content where YouTube's policy requires disclosure;
- sponsored/affiliate relationships must be disclosed appropriately;
- do not make misleading thumbnails/titles that materially misrepresent the content.

Kids-specific:

- no manipulative sales pressure directed at children;
- no child-data collection through Marmalio flows;
- no unsafe challenges or age-inappropriate themes;
- use a separate channel/lane and a dedicated QA checklist.

## Gate 6 — TikTok

- comply with Community Guidelines, IP rules and monetization-program rules;
- do not assume every country/account is eligible for Creator Rewards or other monetization programs — verify inside the actual account before counting projected revenue;
- label AI-generated/significantly AI-edited realistic content where required;
- use original content; Creator Rewards eligibility/performance cannot be based on copied/reused material;
- disclose commercial/affiliate relationships where required;
- do not target children with prohibited/manipulative commercial content.

## Gate 7 — Instagram / Meta

- comply with Meta Community Standards, branded-content rules and IP rules;
- disclose ads/affiliate/sponsored content as required;
- do not rely on copyrighted trending audio outside the rights granted for the specific account/use case;
- verify monetization feature eligibility inside the account because availability varies by country/account/status;
- keep consent/privacy settings consistent with actual analytics and ad tools used.

## Gate 8 — Amazon KDP

Before publication:

- own or hold publishing rights to all text/images/fonts/content;
- disclose AI-generated content in KDP's publication workflow where Amazon requires it (including AI-generated text, images or translations);
- distinguish AI-assisted from AI-generated according to the current KDP definitions;
- do not publish misleading, duplicated, scraped, infringing or low-value books at scale;
- verify title/subtitle/keywords/categories do not mislead customers or abuse trademarks;
- review each book for formatting, factual accuracy, language quality and customer value;
- maintain source/licensing records.

## Gate 9 — Amazon Merch on Demand

Before upload:

- use only original/licensed designs;
- run trademark/IP checks on phrases, logos and graphics;
- avoid prohibited/hateful/illegal/misleading content;
- do not mass-upload near-duplicates or automated junk;
- keep records proving commercial-use rights for design components;
- verify current Merch content policy inside the seller account before each scaled batch.

## Gate 10 — Affiliate marketing / sponsorship

- disclose affiliate links and paid relationships clearly and close to the commercial recommendation;
- do not claim independent/unbiased review if compensation affects the relationship;
- do not fabricate personal experience;
- comply with the affiliate program's brand, bidding, coupon, email and traffic-source rules;
- record commissions and payouts for bookkeeping/tax purposes.

## Gate 11 — Dropshipping / physical products

BLOCKING checklist per product:

- identify the legal seller and supplier relationship;
- order and inspect a sample before scaling;
- verify product safety and applicable EU/German requirements;
- identify manufacturer/importer/responsible economic operator where required;
- verify required warnings, labels, traceability and product information;
- ensure online offer contains required product/manufacturer/safety information where applicable;
- confirm delivery estimates are realistic;
- confirm 14-day consumer withdrawal/return handling and statutory warranty obligations where applicable;
- establish return address/process before sale;
- calculate full landed cost, taxes/duties, refunds, chargebacks and support cost;
- do not sell regulated/high-risk categories until specialist compliance has been completed.

`PROFESSIONAL CHECK`: use product-safety/legal specialist for any category with CE marking, batteries/electronics, toys, cosmetics, food/supplements, medical claims, PPE or other regulated goods.

## Gate 12 — AI / automation governance

- AI agents may draft/research/transform, but do not autonomously publish, spend, create seller accounts, accept binding contracts, launch ads, contact cold leads or make destructive changes without the required human approval.
- Keep a source/evidence trail for factual/legal claims used in products and marketing.
- Avoid presenting AI output as professional legal, medical or financial advice.
- Run copyright/licensing/factual/platform QA before publication.
- Do not feed secrets, customer PII or protected client information into unapproved model surfaces.
